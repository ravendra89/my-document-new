# Steps to Set Up Notification for Leave:
Step 1:
Open the calendar in email.

Select the leave date on the calendar.

Click on "New Items" and then select "Meeting."

Set the title to "Name - PPTO"

Required: Team member's name or email ID.

Remove all content from the dropdown (e.g., Microsoft Teams, etc.).

Remove the location content and mention "OOO" (Out of Office).

Click on "Response Options" and uncheck Request_Responses

show as- should be free


Reminder time - None

Click "Send."

Step 2:
Open the calendar in your email.

Select the leave date on the calendar.

Click on "New Items" and then select "Meeting."

Set the title to "Name - PPTO."

Required field- Name (Ravendra tiwari) or Email id


In the "Show As" field, select "Out of Office."

Remove all content from the dropdown (e.g., Microsoft Teams, etc.).

Remove the location content and mention "OOO" (Out of Office).

Click "Send."

Now it's done.
