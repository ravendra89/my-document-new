# What is Claude?

Claude is an Claude is an AI tool/AI assistant made by Anthropic. It can help with writing, analysis, coding, debug code, research, math, brainstorming, solve problems and general questions — essentially a wide range of text-based tasks.

It’s similar to ChatGPT.

# Anthropic:
Anthropic is the AI safety company that created Claude. Founded in 2021, their focus is building AI systems that are safe and beneficial.

# Cowork: 
Cowork is a desktop app made by Anthropic that lets you use Claude to automate file and task management on your computer — things like organizing files, generating documents, running research, and building workflows. It gives Claude direct access to your local files and a shell environment to do real work on your behalf. It's currently in research preview.
